Bill Splitter

A fintech project: split a bill fairly, and make sure every franc is counted.

1. What it does

You type in a bill in Rwandan francs (RWF) and choose how to split it:

• Equal split: everyone pays the same, with an optional tip.
• Split by what each person ordered: the tip and any shared cost (like a delivery fee) are shared out in proportion to what each person ordered.

Sometimes a bill does not divide into whole francs. This tool never loses or invents a franc: the shares always add up exactly to the total, and the page shows a check line to prove it.

3. Why it matters for fintech

Splitting money is a feature in many payment and mobile money apps: group dinners, shared rent, family contributions, group savings. In all of them the numbers must add up exactly. If an app loses or creates even one franc, its records stop matching the real money, and across thousands of payments that becomes a serious problem. Handling rounding carefully is a basic skill for anyone building money software.

4. What I learned

  • You cannot always divide money evenly: 110,000 ÷ 3 leaves 2 francs over. Someone has to pay 1 RWF more.
  • The "largest remainder" method: round everyone's share down, then give the leftover francs to the people who lost the biggest fraction.
  • Always check that the shares add up exactly to the total. I added a check line that proves it.
  • How to put two different forms on one Django page, and tell them apart with a hidden field.
  • How to read several rows of input with a loop and skip the empty rows.
