Loan and Interest Calculator

A fintech project: see what a loan really costs, month by month.

1. What it does

You type in a loan amount in Rwandan francs (RWF), a yearly interest rate and how many months you will take to repay it. The calculator shows:

• your monthly payment
• the total interest you would pay
• the total you would pay back
• a repayment table showing, for every month, how much of your payment is interest and how much pays off the loan

It assumes equal monthly payments and no extra fees.

3. Why it matters for fintech

Lending is one of the biggest parts of fintech. Mobile loans, buy-now-pay-later and digital lenders all need to work out a clear monthly payment and show the customer what the loan will cost. A tool that explains interest simply helps people borrow with their eyes open. Showing the full repayment table builds trust, because the customer can see exactly where every franc goes.

4. What I learned

The monthly payment comes from this standard loan formula (P = loan amount, r = monthly interest rate, n = number of months):

payment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)−n)
  • Interest is charged on the amount still owed, so early payments are mostly interest and later payments mostly pay off the loan.
  • How to use Python's Decimal for money maths instead of normal decimals.
  • How to build a repayment table with a loop and show it in a Django template.
  • How to handle the 0% interest case separately, so the formula never divides by zero.
  • How to keep a long table usable on a phone with a scrolling box and a show/hide section, with no JavaScript.
