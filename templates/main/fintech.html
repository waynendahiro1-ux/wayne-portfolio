My Focus: Fintech

Where technology meets money, and how it can create real opportunities across Africa.

💡 Why Fintech?

Think about how often you use money in a single day: paying for a snack, sending airtime, splitting a bill with friends. Now imagine all of that happening in seconds from a phone, with no queue, no paperwork, and no bank branch nearby. That is fintech, and here in Kigali I see it working every day.

Fintech means financial technology: mobile money, online payments, digital savings, and apps that make borrowing and investing simpler. For millions of people across Africa, a phone is the first and only way they can reach financial services. When M-Pesa launched in Kenya in 2007, it showed the world that a simple idea built on a basic phone could change how a whole continent moves money.

A good financial app doesn't just move money. It gives people access, trust, and a chance to grow.

What pulls me in is that fintech sits where my interests meet. I love coding, and my A* in IGCSE Computer Science taught me how to break a big problem into small, logical steps. I also love the business side: understanding what people need and building something that solves it. As a basketball captain, I've learned that great results come from teamwork, preparation, and staying calm under pressure, and I think building products works the same way.

I'm still at the start of this journey, and this portfolio is my first step. My goal is to keep learning, build real projects, and one day create a financial tool that people actually use and trust. That is why I'm excited to attend the Africa Fintech Summit.
