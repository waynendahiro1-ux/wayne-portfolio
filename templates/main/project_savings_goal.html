Savings Goal Calculator

A fintech project: plan how long a savings goal will take, or how much to save each month.

1. What it does

You type in a savings target in Rwandan francs (RWF). Then you choose one of two questions:

• "I can save this much each month. How many months will it take?"
• "I want to reach my goal in this many months. How much do I need to save each month?"

You can also enter money you have already saved. This version does not include interest.

3. Why it matters for fintech

Saving goals are a core feature of many fintech apps. Digital savings tools turn a big goal, like school fees or a laptop, into a small monthly step, and that makes saving easier to start and to stick with. Mobile phones are how many people in Rwanda and across Africa reach financial services, so simple, clear tools like this matter.

4. What I learned

  • How to build an HTML form in Django and read what a visitor types.
  • How to check input (empty, negative, letters, too big) and show friendly error messages.
  • How to round up with whole-number maths, because you cannot save for "4.3 months".
  • How to avoid dividing by zero.
  • How to keep a form filled in after the page reloads.
