Mobile Money Wallet Simulator

A fintech project: a demo wallet with fake money, to see how mobile money works.

1. What it does

Mobile money wallets let people store, send and receive money on a phone. This project is a simulator of that idea, built in 4 stages:

  1. Create a demo wallet (ready)
  2. Send money to another demo wallet (ready)
  3. Fees and transaction history (ready)
  4. QR payment request: ask for money with a QR code that another demo wallet scans and pays (ready now)

Everything here is fake. No real money, no real accounts and no personal data. The fees are made-up demo fees, not the fees of any real company. The QR codes only hold a link to this demo page, never real payment details.

3. Why it matters for fintech

Mobile money is one of the most important fintech ideas in Africa. It lets people who do not use a bank send, receive and save money using only a phone. Every mobile money app starts with the same basic building block: a wallet that belongs to a user, has a unique number, and holds a balance that is stored safely and never goes below zero. Sending money adds a second rule: a transfer must take money from one wallet and add the same amount to another, as one single step. If it only half-happens, money disappears or appears from nowhere. Fees add a third rule: the fee must go somewhere, so the books always balance. Providers earn their income from fees, and users need a clear history to see where their money went. QR payments add a fourth: a request must be paid only once, by the right person, before it expires. Shops and street vendors use QR codes so customers can pay without typing a number. Understanding these rules is the first step to understanding how real money systems stay trustworthy.

4. What I learned

  • How to design a database model for a wallet: a nickname, a unique wallet number and a balance in whole francs.
  • How to make a random wallet number and check that no other wallet already has it.
  • How to use a Django session to remember a visitor without a login or any personal data.
  • How to check what the visitor types, and why a wallet balance should never be able to go below zero.
  • How to move money between two wallets in one database transaction, so both balances change together or not at all.
  • How to block bad transfers: wallets that do not exist, sending to yourself, zero amounts and not enough balance.
  • How to lock the wallets during a transfer, so two payments at the same time cannot spend the same money.
  • How to add a fee from a table of amount bands, and why the fee has to be paid to a wallet so no franc disappears.
  • How to build a transaction history from the transfer records, showing money sent (with the fee) and money received.
  • How a QR code works as a link: it only holds a web address, and the real payment happens on the server, so no payment details are ever stored in the picture.
  • How to make sure a payment request can only be paid once, by locking it in the database, and how to expire or cancel old requests.
