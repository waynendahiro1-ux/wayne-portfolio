Mobile Money Wallet Simulator

A fintech project: a demo wallet with fake money, to see how mobile money works.

1. What it does

Mobile money wallets let people store, send and receive money on a phone. This project is a simulator of that idea, built in 4 stages:

  1. Create a demo wallet (ready now)
  2. Send money to another demo wallet (coming next)
  3. Fees and transaction history (coming)
  4. QR payment request (coming)

Everything here is fake. No real money, no real accounts and no personal data.

3. Why it matters for fintech

Mobile money is one of the most important fintech ideas in Africa. It lets people who do not use a bank send, receive and save money using only a phone. Every mobile money app starts with the same basic building block: a wallet that belongs to a user, has a unique number, and holds a balance that is stored safely and never goes below zero. Understanding that building block is the first step to understanding transfers, fees and payments.

4. What I learned

  • How to design a database model for a wallet: a nickname, a unique wallet number and a balance in whole francs.
  • How to make a random wallet number and check that no other wallet already has it.
  • How to use a Django session to remember a visitor without a login or any personal data.
  • How to check what the visitor types, and why a wallet balance should never be able to go below zero.
